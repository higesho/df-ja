The poem is divided into 3 distinct parts: a brief verse paragraph, a quatrain and a couplet.  The Mist of Pages is always written from the perspective of the author.  Use of alliteration, assonance and vivid imagery is characteristic of the form.  Forms of parallelism are common throughout the poem, in that certain lines often share an underlying meaning and have similar grammatical structures.  Each line has 5 feet with a syllable weight pattern of short-long (quantitative iambic pentameter).  Every line of the poem has a medial caesura.  The ending of each line of the poem shares the same rhyme.  The rhyme scheme of the poem is ABAB, where numbers indicate a refrain.
