The Lilac of Gloss is a form of music used during marches and military engagements originating in The Ring of Cremation.  The form guides musicians during improvised performances.  A singer recites a composition of The Strategic Sable Papers while the music is played on a therleth.  The music is melody and rhythm without harmony.  The entire performance should be fiery and is very fast.  The melody has short phrases throughout the form.  Never more than an interval sounds at once.  It is performed in free rhythm.  Throughout, when possible, performers are to make trills and play staccato.  [B]The singer always provides the rhythm.  [B]The therleth always does the main melody.  [B]The Lilac of Gloss has a well-defined multi-passage structure: a theme, an exposition of the theme and a brief recapitulation of the theme.  [B]The theme is to be in whispered undertones.  The therleth covers its entire range from the watery low register to the strident high register and the singer's voice ranges from the low register to the middle register.  The passage is performed using the anam scale.  [B]The exposition is to be moderately soft.  The therleth stays in the strident middle register and the singer's voice covers its entire range.  The passage is performed using the bidok scale.  [B]The recapitulation is to fade into silence.  The therleth covers its entire range from the watery low register to the strident high register and the singer's voice ranges from the middle register to the high register.  The passage is performed using the nicol scale. The Golden Echo is a devotional form of music directed toward the worship of Dastot the Lash originating in The Ring of Cremation which grew out of the performances of The Order of Hammers originally devised by Urist Bootstamp.  The rules of the form are applied by composers to produce individual pieces of music which can be performed.  The music is played on a kubuk, a lorbam and a tosid.  The musical voices join in melody and counterpoint, harmony and rhythm.  The entire performance should be stately and is moderately paced, and it is to be moderately loud.  The melody and counterpoint both have long phrases throughout the form.  The music is broadly layered with chords spanning the range.  The music repeats for as long as necessary.  It is performed using the gozre scale in the lerka rhythm.  Throughout, when possible, composers and performers are to use grace notes, make trills and play legato.  From beginning to end, when improvising or composing, artists should often include a falling-rising melody pattern.  [B]The kubuk always does the main melody.  [B]The Golden Echo has a simple structure: an introduction, a verse and a chorus.  [B]The introduction is to be very soft.  The voice stays in the high register.  Each passage is performed using the gozre scale.  Each passage should be composed and performed using trills, legato and staccato.  The passage has short phrases in the melody, while the counterpoint has long phrases.  This passage typically has some sparse chords.  [B]Scales are constructed from 7 notes dividing the octave.  The tonic note is fixed only at the time of performance.  Every note is named.  [B]The heptatonic scale is thought of as two disjoint chords spanning two perfect fourths.  These chords are named kubuk and lorbam.  [B]The rhythm system is fundamentally polyrhythmic.  There are always multiple rhythm lines, and each of their bars is played over the same period of time, regardless of the number of beats.  The rhythm lines are thought of as one, without a primary-subordinate relationship, though individual lines can be named.	The Golden Echoは、The Ring of Cremationで生まれ、The Order of Hammersの演奏から育ち、もともとUrist Bootstampが考案した、Dastot the Lashへの信仰に向けた信仰のための音楽の形式です。この形式の規則を作曲家が用いて、演奏できる個々の曲が作られます。音楽はkubuk、lorbam、tosidで演奏されます。各声部は、旋律と対位法、和声とリズムで重なります。演奏全体は荘厳に、中くらいの速さで、そしてやや強く演奏されるべきです。旋律と対位旋律はどちらも、形式を通じて長い楽句を用います。音域いっぱいに広がる和音が、幾重にも厚く重ねられます。音楽は必要なだけ繰り返されます。gozre音階を用いて、lerkaのリズムで演奏されます。できるかぎり、作曲者と演奏者は終始装飾音を用い、トリルを奏で、レガートで奏でます。初めから終わりまで、即興や作曲のとき、芸術家はよく下降して上昇する旋律型を含めるべきです。[B]kubukは常に主旋律を担います。[B]The Golden Echoの構成は単純です。導入部、詩節、反復部。[B]導入部は非常に弱く演奏されるべきです。声は高音域にとどまります。どの楽節もgozre音階を用いて演奏されます。どの楽節もトリル、レガート、スタッカートを用いて作られ、演奏されるべきです。この楽節は旋律に短い楽句を、対位旋律に長い楽句を用います。この楽節には、まばらな和音がいくらか置かれるのが普通です。[B]音階は、オクターブを分ける7個の音から組み立てられます。主音が決まるのは、演奏のときだけです。すべての音に名前が付けられます。[B]七音音階は二つの完全四度にまたがる、離れた二つの和音として捉えられます。これらの和音はkubukとlorbamと呼ばれます。[B]リズムの仕組みは、根本からポリリズムです。リズムの線はつねに複数あり、拍の数にかかわらず、どの線の小節も同じ長さの時間で演奏されます。リズムの線は主従のない一つのものとして捉えられますが、線ごとに名前を付けることもできます。
