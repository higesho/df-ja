The Hammer of Dusk is a war dance for a single pair which grew out of the performances of The Guild of Shields originally devised by Urist Bootstamp.  The rules of the form are applied by choreographers to produce individual dances which can be performed.  The dancer performs loosely mingled along an improvised path, moving faster and faster with the music.  The partners rarely make contact.  There is a basic movement sway.  The dance is aggressive, powerful and intense.  The dance is punctuated by the large leaps and the sharp-edged kicks.
